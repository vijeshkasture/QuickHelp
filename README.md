# KaamSetu

### Connecting Work. Connecting People.

KaamSetu is an intelligent hyperlocal workforce matching and coordination platform designed to connect people who need immediate or short-duration services with nearby workers who are available and willing to work.

Instead of functioning as a conventional job listing platform, KaamSetu focuses on the complete local-work lifecycle:

**Request → Recommended Price → Customer Offer → Match → Accept/Reject → Navigate → Complete → Rate → Learn**

---

## 🚀 Problem

Finding a worker for an immediate local task is often dependent on:

- Physical gathering points
- Word of mouth
- Personal contacts
- Informal networks
- Calling multiple workers individually

At the same time, skilled daily-wage and short-duration workers may be available but have no efficient way to discover nearby work opportunities.

KaamSetu aims to reduce this information gap by providing a structured platform for hyperlocal workforce discovery, matching, pricing and coordination.

---

## 💡 Solution

KaamSetu connects customers and local workers through an intelligent matching workflow.

### For Customers

A customer can:

- Select the required service
- Describe the problem
- Share the work location
- Specify urgency
- Receive a recommended price
- Set or increase their offer
- Find suitable available workers
- View worker information
- Track the job
- Rate the worker after completion

### For Workers

A worker can:

- Create a professional profile
- Add skills and services
- Define their service area
- Set availability
- Receive nearby work requests
- View job details and offered price
- Accept or reject work
- Navigate to the customer's location
- Complete jobs
- Build their work history and ratings

## 👥 Role-Based Experience

KaamSetu provides separate experiences for:

### Customer

Find and request local workers.

### Worker

Discover and accept nearby work opportunities.

The application automatically directs users to the appropriate dashboard based on their KaamSetu role.
